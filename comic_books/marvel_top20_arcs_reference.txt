# Top 20 Marvel Story Arcs (1990-2009) — Cancer Metaphor Reference

Curated list of Marvel's top story arcs from the 1990s and 2000s, used as
reference points for cancer-research metaphors in the Comic Metaphor Engine.

Source: curated critical rankings + fan consensus (see citations below).

---

## The 1990s

1. **The Infinity Gauntlet (1991)** — Starlin/Pérez/Lim. Thanos assembles the six
   Infinity Gems, becomes a god. *Multi-modal combination / pan-system integration.*
2. **X-Men: Age of Apocalypse (1995)** — Legion kills Xavier in the past; Apocalypse
   conquers Earth. *Unchecked driver mutation → totalitarian worst-case timeline.*
3. **Marvels (1994)** — Busiek/Ross. Marvel history through photographer Phil
   Sheldon. *Perspective shift; disease through the patient's eyes.*
4. **X-Men: Fatal Attractions (1993)** — Magneto rips adamantium from Wolverine;
   Xavier mind-wipes Magneto. *Forcible removal of an integrated asset; unintended
   consequence of intervention.*
5. **The Clone Saga (1994-1996)** — Is Peter Parker or Ben Reilly (Scarlet Spider)
   the "real" Spider-Man? *Clonal heterogeneity; which subclone drives the disease.*
6. **Onslaught Saga (1996)** — Psychic manifestation of Xavier's dark side attacks
   the MU. *Tumor arising from a cell's own latent program — the shadow of the host.*
7. **Daredevil: The Man Without Fear (1993)** — Miller/Romita Jr. Gritty origin.
   *Facing the origin of fear; early detection / origin story of disease.*
8. **Avengers: Operation: Galactic Storm (1992)** — Kree-Shi'ar war; execution of
   the Supreme Intelligence. *Two-superpower arms race; resource conflict; hard
   decisions with consequences.*
9. **X-Men: The Phalanx Covenant (1994)** — Techno-organic Phalanx assimilate
   mutantkind. *Tumor invasion; hijacking/supplanting normal tissue.*
10. **Rise of the Midnight Sons (1992)** — Horror line launch (Ghost Rider,
    Morbius, Nightstalkers). *Supernatural/atmospheric; confronting the darkness
    within.*

## The 2000s

11. **Ultimate Spider-Man (2000-)** — Bendis/Bagley. Peter Parker in high school.
    *Modernized origin; recontextualizing a known story for a new era.*
12. **Civil War (2006-2007)** — Millar/McNiven. Superhuman Registration Act; Cap
    vs Iron Man. *Compliance vs autonomy; division within the body's defenses.*
13. **House of M (2005)** — Scarlet Witch rewrites reality; "No more mutants."
    *Targeted decimation of a cell population; selective eradication.*
14. **New X-Men (2001-2004)** — Morrison/Quitely. "E is for Extinction."
    *Evolution and extinction pressure; selective sweeps.*
15. **Bendis's Daredevil (2001-2006)** — Secret identity outed. *Exposure of a
    hidden truth; biomarker / detection revealing a concealed disease.*
16. **Winter Soldier (2005-)** — Brubaker/Epting. Bucky returns brainwashed.
    *A trusted defensive asset turned against the host — subverted immune cells.*
17. **Annihilation (2006-2007)** — Giffen. Nova/Silver Surfer vs Annihilus's
    Annihilation Wave. *A spreading wave of destruction — metastasis.*
18. **Secret Invasion (2008)** — Bendis/Yu. Skrulls infiltrate Earth. *Immune
    disguise; enemy already inside.*
19. **Immortal Iron Fist (2006-2007)** — Brubaker/Fraction/Aja. Iron Fist's lore.
    *Precise targeted strikes; single-point intervention.*
20. **Avengers Disassembled (2004-2005)** — Bendis/Finch. Scarlet Witch attacks
    the Avengers; team disbands. *An internal cell turning on the organism;
    tissue disassembly / oncogene-driven disruption.*

## Honorable Mentions
- **Kang Dynasty (2001-2002)** — Time-traveling recurring adversary. *Recurrence;
  adaptive adversary that learns from each encounter.*
- **Welcome Back, Frank (2000)** — Ennis. Punisher's relentless pursuit. *Relentless
  surveillance / immune clearance of a persistent threat.*
- **Mark Waid's FF (2002-2005)** — Whimsical sci-fi. *Optimistic exploration.*
- **Dark Reign (2008-2010)** — Osborn takes over SHIELD. *Subversion of control /
  hostile takeover of governance.*
- **Spider-Man: Blue (2002)** — Loeb/Sale. Gwen Stacy's death. *Grief and loss;
  the cost of a failed intervention.*
