**Source Material**: Fantastic Four #13 (1963), Original Sin (2014)

**The Business Translation**: The Audit Paradox & Performance Monitoring

When you monitor a system, you change it. Employees behave differently under surveillance. The consultant who "just observes" influences decisions by their presence. The data analyst who watches metrics changes what teams optimize for. The board member who "stays out of operations" still casts a shadow over every decision.

**Dimensions**:
* D1 (Bio/Internal): Observer Consciousness
  * Logic: The psychological weight of being watched. Performance anxiety, Hawthorne effect, and the impossibility of pure objectivity.
  * Metric: Behavioral variance under observation - measure productivity delta when monitoring is active vs. passive.

* D2 (Tech/External): Surveillance Architecture
  * Logic: The infrastructure of watching - cameras, analytics dashboards, keystroke loggers. Each layer of observation adds latency and changes the system.
  * Metric: Observation overhead - computational cost and time lag introduced by monitoring systems.

* D3 (Eco/Resources): Information Asymmetry Economics
  * Logic: The Watcher possesses perfect information but cannot act on it. The market inefficiency of non-tradable knowledge.
  * Metric: Knowledge arbitrage potential - value of information × inability to monetize.

* D4 (Cosmic/Limit): Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle
  * Logic: You cannot observe without affecting. Measurement changes the measured. Perfect information is impossible because perfect observation is impossible.
  * Metric: Observer interference coefficient - quantified impact of monitoring on system behavior.
